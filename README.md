# Dubai Rental Market Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard analysing 34,000+ Dubai rental listings to understand what drives rent and where the opportunities are.

   ![Dashboard](EXCEL%20DASHBOARD%20%282%29.png)

## Questions it answers
- How big and how expensive is the rental market?
- Which property types and bedroom counts drive rent?
- Which locations are the most expensive, and where is the supply?
- Do furnished homes rent for more?
- How have asking rents changed over time?

## Key findings
- The typical (median) rent is **AED 145,000**, well below the **AED 213,000** average, because luxury listings pull the average up.
- **Jumeirah Village Circle** has the most listings (3,117) at about **AED 106/sqft**, roughly half of Downtown Dubai (AED 199/sqft).
- Furnished homes rent for about **8% more** on average.
- Rent rises steadily with bedrooms, from about AED 59K for studios to over AED 1.4M at 7 bedrooms.

## Features
- KPI cards: total listings, average and median rent, rent per sqft, average property size
- Charts: rent by property type, bedrooms and rent category; top 10 locations by rent, rent per sqft and listings; furnished vs unfurnished; monthly trend
- Slicers for property type, bedrooms, rent category and furnishing, plus a date timeline
- Key insights panel

## Tools
Excel, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, Timeline, data cleaning, dashboard design

## Notes
- Data: Dubai rental listings (Kaggle dataset)
- Rents are asking rents from listings, not final prices paid
- Locations with very few listings can look extreme and should be read with caution
